04 / OUR STORY

A family legacy
since 1973

Three generations. One passion.

The story of MAD Custom began in 1973 with the vision, hard work and passion of our grandfather, José Rodríguez — affectionately known as “Cheo Colega.”

Cheo laid the foundation for the values that still define our family today: honest work, commitment to our customers, and a genuine passion for the automotive industry. What began as one man's dream became a legacy passed from generation to generation.

IN MEMORY — CARLY

Carly changed the game in Puerto Rico

Carlos Rodríguez — “Carly” — arrived in 2010 with a different vision and an extraordinary passion for the industry. He modernized the business, raised the standard, and led us to becoming the #1 store in wheel and tire sales in Puerto Rico. His impact went far beyond product: he built a culture and an entire experience around automotive customization.

Today Carly rests with the Lord, but his vision, his lessons and his legacy remain alive in every step we take. His absence is deeply felt — his legacy keeps driving us forward.

We remember Carly with love.
